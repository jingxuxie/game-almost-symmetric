\section{Zero-Sum and Statistical Certificates}

For a two-player zero-sum matrix $A$, let $\mathcal Z$ be the zero-sum subspace and define the structured defect $\zsdef=\delta_{G,\mathcal Z}$.  For $x\in\Delta_m,y\in\Delta_n$, the saddle gap
\begin{equation}
\Gap_A(x,y)=\max_i(Ay)_i-\min_j(x^\top A)_j
\label{eq:saddle-gap}
\end{equation}
is the sum of the two player regrets.

\begin{corollary}[Transferred zero-sum certificate]
\label{cor:zs-transfer}
An equilibrium of a $G$-invariant zero-sum surrogate at strategic distance $\delta$ has each original-player regret at most $\delta$ and saddle gap at most $2\delta$.  In particular, a nearest structured surrogate gives gap at most $2\zsdef$.
\end{corollary}

A tighter a posteriori profile is obtained directly from
\begin{align}
\min_{x,y,\alpha,\beta}\quad&\alpha-\beta \label{eq:invariant-gap-lp}\
\text{s.t.}\quad
&Ay\le\alpha\mathbf1,\quad
x^\top A\ge\beta\mathbf1^\top,\nonumber\
&x\in\Delta_m,\ y\in\Delta_n,\ (x,y)\text{ respects }G.\nonumber
\end{align}
For fixed $(x,y)$, the objective is exactly \eqref{eq:saddle-gap}; hence the optimum is the best invariant saddle gap and is at most $2\zsdef$.  In an exactly invariant game, one strategy variable and one best-response constraint per relevant action orbit suffice.

\paragraph{Estimated payoffs.}
Distance to a fixed closed linear class is one-Lipschitz:
\begin{equation}
|\delta_{G,\mathcal C}(\Gamma)-\delta_{G,\mathcal C}(\widetilde\Gamma)|
\le\devdist(\Gamma,\widetilde\Gamma).
\label{eq:defect-stability}
\end{equation}
If $\widehat\Gamma$ is the nearest invariant surrogate of an estimate $\widetilde\Gamma$ with empirical defect $\widehat\delta$, transfer and the triangle inequality give
\[
\operatorname{Reg}_i^\Gamma(\sigma)
\le
\epsilon+\widehat\delta+\devdist(\Gamma,\widetilde\Gamma).
\]
If $P$ payoff means are each estimated from $M$ independent observations in an interval of length one, Hoeffding and a union bound imply, with probability $1-\alpha$,
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{Reg}_i^\Gamma(\sigma)
\le
\widehat\delta+2\sqrt{\frac{\log(2P/\alpha)}{2M}},
\label{eq:sampling-certificate}
\end{equation}
for an exact equilibrium of the projected estimate; the zero-sum saddle gap is at most twice this quantity.  Proofs and variance-adaptive extensions are discussed in the supplement.
