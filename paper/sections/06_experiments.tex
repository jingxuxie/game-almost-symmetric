\section{Experiments}

All studies use explicit games, SciPy/HiGHS, fixed seeds, and one CPU core.  The full sweep uses seed 17, 20 replicates per perturbation scale, 40 replicates per sample count, and repeated timing after warm-up.  Code, raw CSV files, secondary figures, and adversarial regression tests are provided.

\paragraph{Certificate calibration.}
We lift random quotient games by duplicating actions and add strategic Gaussian perturbations.  In zero-sum games, Figure~\ref{fig:results}(a) compares the saddle gap of an equilibrium of the nearest invariant zero-sum surrogate with its certificate $2\zsdef$; every profile is covered up to solver tolerance, while the directly optimized invariant gap is often smaller.  In independently generated general-sum games, Figure~\ref{fig:results}(b) evaluates a welfare-maximizing invariant CE of the projected game.  Its original-game CE violation remains below $\delta_G$.  Directly optimizing welfare over invariant $\delta_G$-CE can improve selection while retaining the same incentive budget.

\paragraph{Correlation and compression.}
Figure~\ref{fig:results}(c) verifies Proposition~\ref{prop:role-correlation}: independent invariant role choices have welfare $n!/n^n$, whereas an invariant CE retains welfare one.  The CE LP matches the analytic construction for every explicitly enumerated instance through $n=5$.  Figure~\ref{fig:results}(d) shows a nested group hierarchy: increasing within-block symmetry reduces the zero-sum representation from 48 action variables to 6 while increasing the structured defect, exposing the predicted compression--fidelity frontier.

\paragraph{Secondary diagnostics.}
The supplement reports the raw-versus-strategic distance separation, the exact $2-2/d$ Reynolds construction, CE profile-orbit compression, zero-sum runtime scaling, finite-sample coverage, and heterogeneous-role welfare.  The test suite audits empty and self-loop incentive graphs, parallel-edge compression, closed critical witnesses, player swaps, CE/CCE transfer, statistical group selection, and invariant profile orbits.  Across 100 generated games, the cycle value, witness mean, reconstructed-surrogate distance, and independent LP objective agree to numerical tolerance.
